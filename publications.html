Software and Research Data

  • D. E. Kędzierski, P. Krasoń, TailBasis: Compatible Bases and Actuator Scheduling, Wolfram Language software package, version 0.1.0, Zenodo (2026). [DOI]

    A computational package accompanying the preprint Compatible Bases and Actuator Scheduling. It provides algorithms for constructing compatible bases, profile-preserving actuator scheduling, and verification routines, together with example notebooks.

  • D. E. Kędzierski, H. Stojałowska, K. Kuhlmann, Database for Real Hyperfields Algorithm, research dataset, Zenodo (2025). [DOI]

    Computational data concerning finite real hyperfields with cyclic positive cones, including numerical results related to their classification and C-characteristics.
