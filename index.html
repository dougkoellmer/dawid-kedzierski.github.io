I am an assistant professor of mathematics at the University of Szczecin. My research explores arithmetic properties of function fields and the rich structure of Drinfeld modules, Anderson t-modules, and related objects. I work with homological and categorical methods motivated by representation theory and algebraic geometry, with particular interest in extension groups, matrix factorizations, and vector bundles. I also study hyperstructures and algorithmic aspects of algebra, aiming to develop computable tools for the analysis of t-modules, hyperfields, and extension spaces. My recent projects combine explicit calculations with conceptual insights, bridging number theory, algebraic geometry, and noncommutative techniques.

Research Interests

  • Arithmetic of function fields and Drinfeld modules
  • Anderson t-modules and extension groups
  • Representation theory of algebras
  • Homological algebra and algebraic geometry
  • Matrix factorizations and vector bundles
  • Hyperfields and hyperstructures
  • Computer algebra and symbolic computation

A list of my published papers, preprints, research software, and datasets is available on the Publications page.

Teaching and Mathematics Education

Alongside my research, I am actively involved in mathematics education at both university and secondary-school levels. My experience includes teaching a broad range of mathematical subjects, supervising bachelor's and master's theses, and preparing future mathematics teachers.

I develop educational materials and teaching methods, including the Remedium calculations programme, designed to address common mathematical misconceptions and strengthen deductive reasoning. I also collaborate with schools, organize teaching activities for university students, and conduct workshops for mathematics teachers.

Details of my teaching activities, supervised theses, educational publications, and teaching projects can be found on the Teaching page.

Curriculum Development and Academic Service

I have been involved in the development and review of university mathematics curricula, learning outcomes, and course syllabi. As a member of the Mathematics Curriculum Team at the University of Szczecin, I contributed to the modernization of bachelor's and master's degree programmes, including mathematics teacher education.

In 2026, I participated in an expert team collaboratively developing draft national curricula for two upper-secondary school subjects: Advanced Mathematics (Matematyka wyższa) and Introduction to Mathematical Reasoning (Wstęp do rozumowań matematycznych). This work was coordinated by the Educational Research Institute (IBE-PIB) on behalf of the Polish Ministry of National Education.
